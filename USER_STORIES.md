# Prioritized User Stories

## Must have
**Customers:** As a customer, I can see lawn-care openings today; know the exact time, price, included/excluded scope, size limit, and confirmation method; upload property photos; provide access/pet details; and receive a booking confirmation.  
**Providers:** As a provider, I can publish an unexpected gap, limit radius/property size, set scope/add-ons/expiry, and choose instant booking or approval.  
**Administrators:** As an admin, I can review provider verification material, approve/reject an application, pause unsafe listings, audit bookings, review disputes, and issue controlled refunds.

## Should have
**Customers:** save providers, cancel under visible policy, track status, receive messages, review five quality dimensions.  
**Providers:** edit/pause/duplicate/cancel openings, see a weekly schedule, add buffers, update job status, and understand fees/payouts.  
**Administrators:** monitor liquidity, cancellations, support queues, expiring documents, and suspicious behavior.

## Could have
Customers can join waitlists and rebook favorites. Providers can publish recurring slots, import calendars, and accept route-friendly suggestions. Admins can manage experiments, service areas, and configurable policies.

## Future
Customers receive live arrival tracking and personalized recommendations. Providers receive demand forecasts, photo-assisted scope suggestions, route optimization, and dynamic discount suggestions. Administrators use explainable risk signals and audited automation—always with human review for adverse action.
